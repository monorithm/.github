# Monorithm

**A real-time intent marketplace** — the marketplace structure of Jiji, refined
to WhatsApp-level simplicity, and boosted by TikTok-like interest distribution,
so anyone can post what they have or need and reach qualified people without
first building an audience.

## What is Monorithm?

Monorithm is a mobile-first marketplace built around *intent*: every post is a
clear expression of what someone has or wants, and the platform's job is to
connect that intent with the right people, fast. It brings together three ideas
most apps keep apart — a structured marketplace, a clean and familiar messaging
experience, and relevance-driven distribution — into one focused product where
honest, useful exchange comes first.
